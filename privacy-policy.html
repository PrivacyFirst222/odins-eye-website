Privacy Policy

ODIN'S EYE, LLC

Applies to our iOS apps Scanist, Enough?, and SeriesGuard, and to this website.

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ODIN'S EYE, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our iOS applications — currently Scanist, Enough?, and SeriesGuard (each, an "App") — and when you visit this website. Our apps are built privacy-first: your documents and records are stored on your device and are not transmitted to us unless you deliberately send something, as described below. By using an App, you agree to this policy.

  1. 1Scope of This Policy

    This policy covers every iOS app published by ODIN'S EYE, LLC on the Apple App Store, including Scanist, Enough?, and SeriesGuard, as well as this website. If a future app's data practices differ, its App Store listing and an updated version of this policy will say so explicitly.

  2. 2The Short Version

    • We do not automatically collect personal information through our apps.
    • No accounts — our apps do not require or create user accounts.
    • No analytics, usage data, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
    • No tracking, no advertising identifiers, no data sale — ever.
    • Everything you create in our apps stays on your device unless you choose to export, share, or back it up, send optional feedback, or explicitly run Scanist's optional AI feature.

    Our App Store privacy labels state "Data Not Collected." That matches ordinary use: the apps do not collect data on their own, and your documents are not sent to us. This policy explains that practice, including the few cases where information leaves your device only because you chose to send it.

  3. 3Information We Collect

    Nothing automatic. Specifically:

    • No identifiers: we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, device identifiers, or any other personally identifiable information as part of using the apps.
    • No usage or diagnostic data: we do not collect app usage logs, analytics events, crash reports, or performance data — automatically or otherwise.
    • No location, contacts, or browsing data.
    • No data from Apple frameworks: we do not read data from Apple Health, iCloud, or other system services.

    Our apps do not connect to servers we operate in order to store documents, plans, or records. We do not operate servers that receive that content.

    Optional in-app feedback

    If an app offers optional in-app feedback and you choose to send it, only the text and details you decide to include are transmitted, so we can read them and respond. We do not add automatic telemetry, analytics, or usage data to that message, and we do not attach your documents. Document contents are not sent unless you type them into the message yourself. If you do not submit feedback, nothing is transmitted.

  4. 4Information You Store in Our Apps

    The content you create belongs to you and stays on your device:

    Scanist: document scans, imported photos and PDFs, text produced by on-device text recognition, folder organization (including nested folders in the private Vault), signatures you save, and the edits you make are stored in the app's local storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded or transmitted to us by default. If you later choose an optional AI action, only the page content you select for that action is sent, and it goes directly from your device to the provider you picked — see Section 6. Scanist's Erase tool visually edits a page image and is not secure redaction; see Section 12.

    Enough?: the financial information you enter — assets, income, spending, inflation and return assumptions, retirement ages, survivor and legacy assumptions, future events, saved scenarios, comparison sets, and documents in the app's vault — is stored exclusively on your device. All plan calculations, "Show Math" tables, and Need analyses are computed locally. We never see your financial information.

    SeriesGuard: the recordkeeping and accounting information you enter — Series LLCs, parent entities, protected series, members, bank/cash accounts, assets, liabilities, transfers, money received and spent, and the supporting documents and notes you attach — is stored exclusively on your device. We never see your records.

  5. 5Device Permissions and Why We Request Them

    Our apps request only the iOS permissions needed for features you choose to use. Each permission is requested at first use, is optional, and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings without losing access to the rest of the app.

    • Camera (Scanist): used solely to scan documents. Images are processed and stored on your device. The camera is never accessed in the background.
    • Photo Library (Scanist): used solely to import the photos you select. We use the iOS picker, which shares only the specific items you choose.
    • Face ID / Touch ID (Scanist, Enough?, and SeriesGuard): requested only if you turn on an optional in-app lock, such as Scanist's Vault Lock. Authentication is performed by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Biometric data is managed entirely by iOS and the Secure Enclave — it is never available to us or to our apps, and we never store it. Where the app offers it, you may use an app password instead.
    • Files access (Scanist, Enough?, and SeriesGuard): used only when you explicitly import or export documents, backups, or reports through the iOS document picker or share sheet, including a Scanist backup you save to a folder you choose in Apple Files.

  6. 6On-Device Processing

    Ordinary processing stays on your device. Text recognition (OCR) in Scanist uses Apple's on-device Vision framework and can recognize text in multiple languages. Search across that text also stays on the device. All financial modeling in Enough? — including compound-growth projections, run-out age, target rate of return, and maximum spending calculations — runs locally on your device. SeriesGuard keeps the records you enter on the device. We do not send your documents, plans, or records to a cloud service for processing unless you explicitly use Scanist's optional AI features described below.

    Scanist's optional bring-your-own-key (BYOK) AI features

    • These features are off by default and never run automatically.
    • They are available only if you choose to add your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), or xAI (Grok).
    • API keys are stored locally in the iOS Keychain on your device and are never transmitted to ODIN'S EYE, LLC.
    • Only when you explicitly trigger an AI action — AI Text Cleanup or AI Title Suggestions — does Scanist send the selected page content directly from your device to the provider you chose. That request uses an ephemeral session: no cookies and no caching. The provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to what you send. ODIN'S EYE, LLC never relays, views, or stores those requests or keys.

  7. 7Exports, Backups, and Sharing You Initiate

    • From Scanist you may export a PDF (image-only, searchable, or password-protected), an editable Word document (.docx), or JPEG/PNG images, as a single page or in bulk. From Enough? you may export reports as text, PDF, or Excel files. Exports exist only where you choose to save or send them.
    • Scanist can create a password-protected encrypted backup and restore it from any folder you choose in Apple Files, including iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or a folder on the device. The backup is encrypted on your device with your password before it leaves the app. We never receive a copy and cannot decrypt or recover it for you — keep your backup password safe.
    • If you save exports or backups to a third-party service (for example iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or email), that service's privacy policy applies to the copy you placed there. We are not responsible for the practices of services you choose.

  8. 8Data Sharing and Disclosure

    We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. Your documents, plans, and records are not in our possession:

    • We do not sell, rent, trade, or disclose the contents of the apps — we do not hold those contents.
    • Our apps contain no third-party analytics, advertising, or social SDKs.
    • We use no data brokers. We do not act as a relay for app data. The only third party that receives page content is an AI provider you personally select, and only in the optional Scanist case described in Section 6, sent directly from your device.
    • A feedback message you voluntarily submit is used to respond to you and to improve the apps. We do not sell it.
    • Because we do not hold your documents or records, we have nothing of that kind to produce in response to legal requests concerning app content. A feedback message you sent us is the exception, and we would handle any request for it as the law requires.

  9. 9App Store Purchases

    Our apps are sold through the Apple App Store. All payment processing is handled by Apple; we never receive your payment details, billing address, or Apple ID information. Apple provides us only with aggregated, anonymized sales statistics. For details on how Apple handles purchase data, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

  10. 10Tracking and Advertising

    We do not track you. Our apps do not access the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), do not engage in cross-app or cross-site tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, and therefore never display the ATT permission prompt. There are no ads in our apps.

  11. 11Data Retention and Deletion

    Because your documents and records live on your device, retention and deletion of that content are entirely in your hands:

    • Retention: your content is retained on your device only for as long as you keep it. We do not retain your documents, plans, or records on any server.
    • Deletion in-app: you can delete individual documents, scans, scenarios, or vault items at any time from within each app, or clear all app data from the app's settings.
    • Deleting the app from your device permanently removes all of its local data.
    • Backups you created are under your control — delete them from wherever you stored them.
    • Feedback you sent: if you want a feedback message deleted, contact us and we will delete it from our correspondence.
    • Withdrawing consent: you may revoke any permission (camera, photos, Face ID, files) at any time in iOS Settings, and stop all use of the apps by deleting them.

  12. 12Data Security

    Local app data is protected by iOS Data Protection when your device is locked. That protection is provided by iOS. We do not add a separate layer of disk encryption, and Vault Lock is not encryption at rest.

    Scanist's Vault Lock is an in-app access lock. If you turn it on, you can require Face ID, Touch ID, or a password before the Vault opens, and the app shows a privacy shield in the app switcher so page contents are not left on screen there. Vault Lock controls access inside the app. It does not separately encrypt data stored on disk.

    When you export a Scanist backup, the app encrypts that file with XChaCha20-Poly1305 using the password you choose, before the file leaves the app. Keep that password. We never receive the backup and cannot recover the file or a forgotten password.

    Scanist's Erase tool visually edits a page image — for example, to cover something you do not want to see on the page. It is not secure redaction. Material you erase may still exist in the underlying image, in an earlier version of the page, or in a form software could recover. Do not rely on Erase to permanently remove sensitive information.

    No security measure is infallible. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode and keeping iOS up to date. Our apps are consumer tools and are not HIPAA-covered services.

  13. 13Your Privacy Rights

    Privacy laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and similar laws grant rights to access, correct, delete, and port personal data, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal data. Your documents, plans, and records are already entirely under your control on your device, and we have no copy to access, delete, or port. If we hold a message you sent us — for example through the website contact form or optional in-app feedback — contact us and we will address it promptly. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising privacy rights.

  14. 14Children's Privacy

    Our apps are not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The apps do not collect personal information through ordinary use. If you are a parent or guardian with concerns, please contact us.

  15. 15International Users

    Your documents, plans, and records stay on your device wherever you are in the world. We do not transfer that content to our servers, so we do not move it across borders. Information leaves your device only when you choose an action that sends it: an export or backup you save, a feedback message you submit, or — if you turn the feature on and trigger it — a Scanist AI request sent directly to the provider you selected.

  16. 16This Website

    This website (odinseyeapps.com) is a static site. It sets no cookies and loads no third-party analytics, tracking scripts, or external font services. Fonts are served from this website itself. If you use the contact form, the name, email address, and message you submit are delivered to us by our form processor, Formspree, Inc. (see the Formspree Privacy Policy), and are used solely to respond to your inquiry — never for marketing, and never shared or sold. Our hosting providers may keep standard, short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operational purposes, as virtually all web hosting does.

  17. 17Changes to This Policy

    If our practices change, we will update this policy, revise the "Last Updated" date above, and — for material changes affecting an app — provide notice in that app or in its App Store release notes before the change takes effect. The current version will always be available at this page.

    Questions about this policy or anything privacy-related? We answer swiftly.
