MyOwnScanner

Productivity · Privacy-First

A privacy-focused document scanner built in SwiftUI. Scan, import, organize, edit, and export your documents — everything stays on your device unless you choose to send it elsewhere.

  • Private Vault with Face ID or app password lock
  • On-device text recognition
  • Photo & PDF import, configurable scan filters
  • PDF & image export
  • Encrypted backup & restore

Enough?

Finance · Planning

A financial planning math app that answers the question behind every plan: are your assets, income, and spending enough to carry you through the age you choose?

  • Scenario inputs for assets, income, spending, inflation, returns, retirement ages & future events
  • Plan results: run-out age, ending balance, target return, max spending & heir balance
  • "Show Math" tables and compound-growth modeling — no black box
  • Need sliders to weigh return vs. spending trade-offs
  • Saved scenarios, comparisons, document vault & Insights guidance

SeriesGuard

Finance · Series LLC Records

SeriesGuard Ledger is a recordkeeping and accounting organizer for Series LLCs. It guides every event through the correct flow, links related records, and keeps members, accounts, liabilities, and transactions tied to the right entity — all in one place.

  • Track Series LLCs, parent entities & protected series together
  • Manage members, bank/cash accounts, assets & liabilities
  • Record money received, money spent & transfers through the right source-of-truth flow
  • Tie every record to the correct LLC, parent section, series, member or account
  • Attach supporting documents & notes, kept separate from accounting events
  • Cleaner, more defensible records for reports, reconciliation & audits
